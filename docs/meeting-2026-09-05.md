## Suggested research workflow contract

Approved PRD → confirm research scope → competitor and reference lanes in parallel → verify claims and score evidence → assemble comparison/review screen → human feedback → targeted repair → approved JSON handoff to downstream design.

Maintain separate statuses for observed support, explicit evidence of absence, conflicting evidence, and unknown. Use source URL, acquisition time, product/version when known, page/screen/flow context, quote or visual proof, and limitation per claim. Compare UX only where task/platform/context are comparable. Keep preference scores separate from evidence reliability.

Previous user requirements still apply: about five conversational questions per review batch; up to three automatic repair attempts and five human-directed rounds before escalation; detailed stage-specific criteria; JSON outputs and approval history; human approval bound to the shown revision.
