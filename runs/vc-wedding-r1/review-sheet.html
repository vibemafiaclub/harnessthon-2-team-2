Concepts in this batch

  • Blush Letterpress (concept-blush-serif) — Classic paper-invitation warmth: soft blush and cream ground, deep warm-brown text, generous rounded cards with barely-there shadows. Serif-flavored display moments, floral-adjacent restraint. Aims squarely at the 'trustworthy enough for parents' generation' hint - it reads like a printed invitation the whole family recognizes.
    Differs because: Warmest and most traditional of the three: light warm palette and soft large radii, versus Midnight Garden's dark cool-toned formality and Sunlit Editorial's sharp near-zero radii and high-contrast modern layout. Mood is gentle/romantic where the others are ceremonial or crisp.
  • Midnight Garden (concept-midnight-garden) — Evening-ceremony formality: deep green-black ground with ivory text and champagne-gold accents, medium radii, luminous glow-edged surfaces. Celebratory in a candlelit, upscale-venue way - the invitation as a keepsake. Dark-first design that still keeps body text high-contrast for older guests.
    Differs because: The only dark, cool-temperature concept: inverted palette and gold-on-ink luxury versus the light warm ground of Blush Letterpress and the bright airy white of Sunlit Editorial. Density is mid, mood is formal/ceremonial rather than gentle or playful, and shadows are glows instead of drop shadows.
  • Sunlit Editorial (concept-sunlit-editorial) — Bright modern-magazine energy for couples who want festive over frilly: pure white ground, near-black ink text, one saturated coral primary and marigold accent, squared-off corners and flat color blocks. Information-forward - schedule, map, and RSVP presented as bold, scannable modules that stay crisp on low-end phones.
    Differs because: Sharpest and most contemporary: near-zero radii and flat no-shadow blocks versus the soft rounded warmth of Blush Letterpress and the glowing dark surfaces of Midnight Garden. Highest perceived density and color saturation of the three; celebratory-playful mood where the others are romantic or formal.

Rationale summary

The PRD's competing hints — trustworthy enough for parents' generation, festive over frilly, and a keepsake-quality evening option — map naturally onto three maximally separated brand overlays: warm-light-traditional (Blush Letterpress), dark-formal-luxurious (Midnight Garden), and bright-flat-contemporary (Sunlit Editorial), differing on every overlay axis (palette temperature and value, radius scale, shadow model, mood). The single representative screen is the full invitation scroll at 390x844 because it exercises all nine PRD content blocks — hero through RSVP, transfer deep-links, and share footer — so reviewers judge each direction against the real content density and Korean copy it must carry. Each file pairs that screen with a style tile (palette, type scale, four-state buttons, card) so an approved direction can be applied to the remaining screens without re-deriving tokens.
