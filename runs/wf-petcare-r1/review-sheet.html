근거 요약

Three structurally distinct clickable wireframes for prd-petcare-booking-v1, round 1 (390x844). Variant intents: hub ('Hub-and-spoke with role-split tabs') organizes the app around a tab-root home with an owner/sitter role switch, spreading the flows across spokes so status is glanceable from the hub; wizard ('Linear wizard funnel') commits to a strictly linear owner funnel (search intent → results → profile → 3-step request wizard → chat gate → pay → status/report) plus a 4-stage sitter pipeline, one primary CTA per screen; thread ('Single-scroll discovery + booking-as-thread') collapses discovery into one anchored scroll with sheet overlays and makes a single booking thread the shared surface for chat, state changes, payment, and the care report. AI representative recommendation: recommendedVariantId 'wizard' — the wizard maps 1:1 onto both PRD coreFlows (owner funnel reproduces flow-find's five steps exactly; the sitter pipeline mirrors flow-sitter), giving the best task efficiency and zero-ambiguity for first-time users via one primary CTA plus progress per screen; the scope checklist and read-only review step prevent disputes, and the payment button locked behind the '수락됨' system event makes the requested/accepted/in-progress/done states visibly unambiguous, while hub dilutes the flows across tab spokes with a hidden role switch and thread interleaves owner and sitter action chips in one stream with low-discoverability inline actions — riskier as the representative structure for a calm, anxiety-reducing first-time experience. Audience rule: 내부용 - 클라이언트 비노출, 디자이너는 대표 구조 선택만.
