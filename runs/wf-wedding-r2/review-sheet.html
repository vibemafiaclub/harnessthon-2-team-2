근거 요약

The prototype keeps the PRD's two core flows as two disjoint clickable loops (builder: template-gallery → editor → preview → publish; guest: invitation → RSVP), merging PRD steps that share one surface (editor absorbs details+photos+greeting; guest page absorbs greeting/venue/transport) to stay at 6 screens. A hash-based router with one section per screen keeps every edge a real navigable link, including a flow-map overview whose SVG boxes deep-link to each screen. Low-fi grayscale styling and dashed placeholders deliberately defer brand direction to the visual-concept lane.
